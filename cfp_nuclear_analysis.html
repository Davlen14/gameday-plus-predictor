📊 Analysis Methodology
🧮 Connection Strength Formula
Connection Strength = (Direct H2H × 5) + (Common Opponents × 1)

Direct head-to-head matchups receive 5x weight since they provide the most reliable comparison data between teams.

🎯 Network Density Calculation
Network Density = (Actual Connections / Possible Connections) × 100%

With 12 teams, maximum possible connections = 66 (12 × 11 ÷ 2). Current density shows how interconnected the CFP field is.

🏆 Relationship Categories
  • Direct: Teams played each other in 2025 season
  • Strong Network: 4+ mutual opponents (high comparative data)
  • Moderate Network: 2-3 mutual opponents (moderate comparative data)
  • Weak Network: 1 mutual opponent (limited comparative data)
🔬 Why This Matters for Predictions

Teams with stronger network connections have more reliable comparative data for predictions. Direct matchups provide the gold standard, while common opponents allow for transitive analysis. The visualization helps identify which matchups have the most predictive power based on available competitive intelligence.
